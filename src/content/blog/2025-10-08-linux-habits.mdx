# Linux Habits That Compound

Most of my Linux wins are boring: tiny habits repeated daily.

## The shortlist

- keep a clean dotfiles repo
- alias the top ten commands I use
- learn one new flag per week
- read system logs before blaming the app

## The result

The system starts to feel predictable. When something breaks, the fixes are
shorter and the lessons stick.
