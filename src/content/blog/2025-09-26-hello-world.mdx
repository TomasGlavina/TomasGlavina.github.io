# Hello World

Hi, welcome to my blog.

I built this space to write about software engineering, Linux, and the things I
learn while studying and building projects.

Most posts here will be practical and technical:

- engineering notes from topics I am currently learning
- short breakdowns of project architecture and tradeoffs
- Linux workflow habits, tooling, and debugging lessons

I write mainly to retain knowledge better and to share what worked (and what did
not) along the way.

If you are into backend systems, clean architecture, terminal-first workflows,
or just figuring things out one topic at a time, you will probably feel at
home here.

Thanks for reading.
